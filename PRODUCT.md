## Users

Three audiences, all confirmed: thesis students (Ingegneria Informatica, Ingegneria dell'Informazione, Medicina Digitale) looking for a lab to join; industrial partners looking for R&D and technology-transfer collaborators; researchers seeking scientific collaboration. Language: English first, with a toggle to Italian.

## Product Purpose

Landing page for KnowMIS (Knowledge Management and Information Systems), a laboratory of the Dipartimento di Ingegneria dell'Informazione ed Elettrica e Matematica Applicata (DIEM), Università degli Studi di Salerno. The lab studies how people, organisations and intelligent systems turn situation awareness into effective decisions. Success: a visitor understands what the lab does, which group fits them, who leads it, and how to reach it.

## Positioning

Situation Awareness is the lab's scientific identity: "from data to understanding, from awareness to decision". Two stable research groups: KnowMIS-SA (Situation Awareness, scientific direction prof. Giuseppe D'Aniello) and KnowMIS-DSS (Decision Support Systems, scientific direction prof. Matteo Gaeta). The user wants particular emphasis on the SA line. Matteo Gaeta is the lab's responsible director (the university page labels him "Responsabile Scientifico").

## Capabilities and Constraints

- SA: cognitive and computational modelling of situations; sensor networks and data processing; situation reasoning and understanding; visualisation and analysis of situations; Granular Computing for Cognitive Situation Management. Domains: Smart City, Logistics, E-Learning, Safety & Security, Enterprise.
- DSS: hybrid adaptive decision architectures; Group Decision Making with Computational Intelligence (Fuzzy Logic); collective-perception analysis in Cyber-Physical-Social Systems; business intelligence and analytics.
- Teaching: thesis work on SA and DSS.
- Technology transfer: spin-off UrbanMIS (urbanmis.com).
- Equipment: 8 HP workstations, 1 HP server, 1 HP Z8 Fury G5 workstation.
- Address: Università degli Studi di Salerno, Via Giovanni Paolo II, 132, 84084 Fisciano (SA).
- No lab email or phone is published; contact goes through the UniSA rubrica pages of the members. Do not invent contacts.
