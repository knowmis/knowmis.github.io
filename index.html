From data to understanding. From awareness to decision.

KnowMIS, the Knowledge Management and Information Systems laboratory of the University of Salerno, studies how people, organisations and intelligent systems turn situation awareness into effective decisions.

Director · Matteo Gaeta

Work with the lab See the research

Situation Awareness is where good decisions begin.

A core theme of KnowMIS: making complex environments interpretable, so that people and systems decide better. One scene, the Gulf of Salerno, at three resolutions: perceive, comprehend, project.

Perceive

Sensor networks and data processing gather what an environment emits and turn raw streams into signals worth attending to.

Comprehend

Cognitive and computational models of situations, with reasoning and visualisation that give signals meaning.

Project

Granular Computing for Cognitive Situation Management supports prediction and decision making.

Two research groups, one question.

KnowMIS-SA Situation Awareness

Scientific direction: Prof. Giuseppe D’Aniello

Cognitive and computational modelling of situations; sensor networks and data processing; situation reasoning and understanding; visualisation and analysis of situations. Built on Granular Computing, AI, Computational Intelligence, Cognitive Science and Human Factors.

  • Smart City
  • Logistics
  • E-Learning
  • Safety & Security
  • Enterprise

KnowMIS-DSS Decision Support Systems

Scientific direction: Prof. Matteo Gaeta

Intelligent decision support in Cyber-Physical-Social Systems: hybrid adaptive decision architectures, group decision making with computational intelligence such as fuzzy logic, collective-perception analysis, and business intelligence and analytics.

  • Adaptive decision architectures
  • Group decision making
  • Fuzzy logic
  • Collective perception
  • Business intelligence & analytics
